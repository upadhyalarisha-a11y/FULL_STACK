Headline

New green transit network promises shorter commutes and safer streets

By Anna Ramirez · City reporter

The city council voted unanimously to approve a comprehensive transit plan that will add dedicated bus lanes, protected cycling corridors, and increased late-night service to underserved neighborhoods.

The initiative is designed to reduce peak-hour congestion by an estimated 20% and improve air quality across the metropolitan area. Officials say work will begin next spring and include community-led planning sessions.

Supporters say the plan will make travel safer for pedestrians and commuters while encouraging more residents to leave cars at home.

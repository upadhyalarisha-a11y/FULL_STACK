City Council approves new green transit plan to reduce commute times

By Anna Ramirez

In a landmark vote, the council approved a multimodal transit proposal designed to link neighborhoods with faster bus lanes, protected bike routes, and expanded late-night service. Supporters say the changes aim to cut traffic congestion by 20% and provide safer travel for commuters.

The plan also includes investments in electric vehicle charging infrastructure and a public awareness campaign centered on sustainable commuting options. Residents will see a phased rollout beginning next spring.
